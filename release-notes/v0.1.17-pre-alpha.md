**0.1.17 is about two things: recipes that taste good, and a new look.**

## 😋 Tasty first
Plans used to pick whatever fit your calories best, so plain, unseasoned recipes and odd dishes slipped in. Now flavour counts as much as fit.
- **Star ratings count, and more so with more reviews.** A 4.8★ recipe that 900 people rated beats a 5★ recipe with one rating.
- **A flavour score (0–10) for every recipe.** It counts the kinds of seasoning a recipe has: garlic and onion, spices, herbs, something sour (lemon, lime, vinegar), heat, savoury depth (soy, miso, parmesan) and salt. Plain recipes are pushed down.
- **Familiar dishes first:** tacos, curries, stir-fries, chili, bowls and pasta, ahead of strange combinations, unless lots of people loved them.
- **Trusted, free, flavour-focused recipe sites are searched first and given more time:**
  - Minimalist Baker, Love and Lemons, RecipeTin Eats, Cafe Delites, Seonkyoung Longest, Swasthi's, BBC Good Food, Jamie Oliver, Kalyn's Kitchen, iFoodReal and Chef Savvy.
  - Plainer public-health sites are searched last.
  - Three sites whose terms forbid automated reading are no longer used.
  - Still at most 3 meals a week from any one site.
- **"Make it tastier":** a plain recipe now suggests 2–3 near-zero-calorie upgrades that suit it, like garlic, lemon, chili flakes, herbs, smoked paprika or a splash of soy. One tap adds the upgrade to the ingredients and a step to the method. Its few calories are counted.
- **Thumbs up or down on any recipe.** A thumbs down keeps the dish out of your plans, and a thumbs up brings it back more often. This is saved only on your device.
- **The local AI (LM Studio) follows the same rules.** It is asked for well-known, full-flavoured dishes, and a bland one is written again. It no longer forces one cuisine on all of a day's meals, which used to give Japanese breakfasts with fish flakes.
- **The result, on 4 test weeks (84 meals):**
  - Average flavour score went from 5.4 to 6.2.
  - Bland meals went from 24 to 13.
  - Meals from trusted sites went from 15 to 44.
  - Meals with a star rating went from 29% to 44%.

## 🎨 A new look
- **Recipe photos.**
  - Plans, the Cookbook and the recipe page show each recipe's own photo from its website, with a big photo at the top of the recipe.
  - **Settings → Appearance → Recipe photos** turns them off, and then nothing is downloaded.
  - A recipe without a photo shows its meal's colours.
- **Today shows your next meal big, as "Up next".**
- **On a computer, Nourish uses the whole window:**
  - A sidebar replaces the tab bar.
  - Today shows your numbers beside your meals.
  - The Plan shows its days side by side.
  - The grocery list has two columns.
  - Recipes open as a centred card.
- **Easier to read:**
  - Captions and small labels are bigger.
  - Grey text is darker in both the dark and light themes, so it's at least 4.5 times the contrast of its background (the WCAG level for normal text).
- Drawn thumbs-up and thumbs-down icons, and a styled "Make it tastier" panel.
- Dark stays the default, all themes, accents and fonts work as before, and the Spacing, Corners, Increase contrast and Coloured amounts options apply to the new design.
- The design system is written down in `DESIGN.md`.

## 🔧 Fixes found by the plan audit
- Canned pumpkin is counted as pumpkin, not butternut squash, and bean sprouts as bean sprouts, not cabbage.
- "Jalapeño pepper" is a jalapeño, not black pepper or a bell pepper.
- Soya milk is soy milk, so it's fine in a vegan plan.
- When most of a recipe's time is waiting, like freezing fruit for a smoothie, the plan shows the hands-on minutes.
- Saffron counts as a luxury ingredient, like truffle: it's left out unless the budget is "No limit".
- A pie whose name doesn't say what's in it, like "Cowboy pie", is left out rather than risk a dessert at dinner.
- A recipe description no longer starts with stray punctuation, like "). I use…".

## Not yet
- PDFs still can't be read on Android (EPUB and Word can).
- Scanned books are only read on iPhone.
- Recipes saved before 0.1.17 get their photo when Nourish next refreshes its recipe library.
