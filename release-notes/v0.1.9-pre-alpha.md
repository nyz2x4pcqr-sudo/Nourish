> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

## 🔁 No more repeated meals
- **No recipe appears twice in a plan.** The same dish from two sites, or with a slightly different name ("Gochujang Chicken Bowls" / "Easy Gochujang Chicken Rice Bowl"), counts as the same.
- Meals from your **last two weeks of plans** are avoided too, except favourites (saved to your Cookbook or rated "loved it").
- **Why meals repeated before:** when a meal couldn't be found or written, Nourish reused the closest recipe from earlier in the week. That fallback is gone. Now it uses an unused recipe, then one of Nourish's own, then the AI. A meal is never repeated.
- New option in **Settings → My schedule: Allow leftovers** (off unless you turn it on): cook once and eat twice, so a dinner can come back as the next day's lunch.

## 🍳 214 recipes built in
- Nourish now has its own original recipes: **67 breakfasts, 74 lunches and 73 dinners** from more than 20 cuisines.
  - Vegetarian and vegan options are included, with enough for a week on any diet.
  - Each one has real seasoning, realistic portions and short beginner steps, with nutrition worked out by the app's calculator.
- They work **offline** and compete with web recipes on equal terms.

## 🔎 Smarter recipe search
- **Searches by meal:** breakfast words like eggs, oats, yogurt or smoothie, inside each site's own breakfast category; lunch and dinner the same way.
- **Why searches found drinks, desserts and sauces:** the sites' search listed their *newest* posts that mention the word anywhere, so "eggs" brought back a coconut cake. Searches now ask for the best match by recipe title.
- Drinks, desserts, sauces and condiments are turned away using the recipe's own category.
- Each site only gets searches that suit it (no meat searches on vegetarian sites, for example).
- Every meal gets searched until it has at least 21 good recipes (3 a day for a week), or about 40 seconds pass.
- **Your recipe library grows:** every recipe read is kept for a year (up to 1,200), so later plans start with a big pool and work offline.
  - It refreshes quietly in the background while the app is open, **on Wi-Fi only**.
- A site that refuses Nourish (403/405) is left alone for a week, or a month if it keeps refusing. Blocks are respected, never worked around.
- No single website can fill the pool.
- **Sites:**
  - Four sites gave links but no recipes before. Fixed, and now giving recipes: RecipeTin Eats, Love and Lemons, Seonkyoung Longest, Cafe Delites.
  - Nine sites refuse automated readers on every try, so they were removed: Budget Bytes, Cookie and Kate, Ambitious Kitchen, Pinch of Yum, Hot Thai Kitchen, Mexico in My Kitchen, The Mediterranean Dish, FeelGoodFoodie (it was also downloading a 1.7 MB file on every plan) and Isabel Eats.
- A disliked side ingredient is swapped instead of losing a good recipe (mushrooms → zucchini, cilantro → parsley). Allergies and your diet are never swapped.

## 🤖 The small phone AI does small jobs
- Real recipes and Nourish's own come first. The AI only writes a whole recipe when nothing else fits.
- Its main jobs now are small ones: choosing between real recipes, suggesting a swap for an ingredient you don't eat, and a one-line description on the recipe.
- **Fixed in code instead of asking again:**
  - Labels like "Lunch for Jeff – Day 7" become real dish names.
  - Description "steps" are removed.
  - Unused herbs and toppings are worked into the steps.
  - Extra steps are joined.
  - The AI's own calories are thrown away and worked out from the ingredients.
- Only real problems make it try again: a cut-off answer, the wrong kind of meal, an avoided food, or far too long. One step or ingredient too many is no longer a reason.
- Your name and the day are no longer in what the AI is asked.

## 🎯 Targets without an AI
- A plan made with **no AI model at all** keeps to your calories (within a few percent in tests), protein, meal types and My schedule.
- A last check makes sure no day is more than 10% off. The "2,400 kcal against 1,500" days can't happen any more.

## ⏱️ Clear times and portions
- **No more "— min":** when a recipe gives no time, it's worked out from the steps and shown as "about 20 min".
- **Hands-on time is shown apart from waiting:** "10 min + overnight" instead of "250 min", with a **Make ahead** label. Your time limits count hands-on time.
- When a portion is sized to fit your day, cards and recipes say so (**"1.25 servings"**), and the amounts match. That's why the same recipe can show different calories on different days.

## 📁 Recipe folder in LiveContainer
- Settings → Recipes now says plainly why the folder is buried: Nourish is running inside another app. It shows the exact path, and **Add files** is the easy way in.

## Not yet
- Recipe sites can block any phone at any time. When they do, Nourish uses its own recipes and your library.
- PDFs still can't be read on Android.
