## ✨ What's new in 0.3.0

- **🎨 A whole new look**
  - Redesigned from top to bottom: cleaner cards, real icons, a calorie ring, a day strip with **Today** marked, and smooth animations.
  - **Dark, Light or Auto** theme, 6 accent colours and 3 text sizes, under **Settings → Appearance**.
  - The phone apps change the status bar to match the theme. *(Needs the new APK/IPA from this release.)*
- **💬 Chat that actually does things**
  - Say **"make me a meal plan"** and the plan goes straight into your **Plan** tab and grocery list.
  - Say **"swap Wednesday dinner for something vegetarian"** and only that meal changes.
  - Each recipe also has a **Swap meal** button.
  - Choose the chef's style and reply length, or turn this off, under **Settings → Chat**.
- **🔄 Every device shows the same thing**
  - Your settings, plan, grocery list and chat are now kept on your PC and synced to every phone and browser. Change something on your phone and it shows on your PC a few seconds later, and the other way round.
  - API keys are saved on your PC and never sent back to your phone.
- **⚙️ More settings**: your name, which tab opens first, which day the week starts, show or hide nutrition, reduced motion, foods you love and avoid, default recipe source, and grocery options. There are now 9 settings pages.
- **🛒 Better grocery list**: a progress bar, your own extra items, hide ticked items, and **Share/Copy** the list.

## 📥 Updating
- **Nourish.exe 0.2.0:** open **Settings → Updates → Download & install**. Your plan and settings are kept.
- **First time after updating:** the first device to connect sends its plan and settings to the PC. Other devices then take the PC's copy. If a phone had different settings before, it switches to the PC's.
- **Phone apps:** the old apps keep working (they show Nourish from your PC). Install the new APK/IPA only if you want the status bar to follow the light theme.

## 📥 Install

**Windows (the PC that runs Nourish)**
1. Download **Nourish.exe** and put it in its own folder. It saves your data next to itself in `nourish-data.json`.
2. Double-click it and keep the black window open. Your browser opens the app.
3. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**. The app isn't code-signed yet.

You also need an AI, for example [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai) with a model loaded and its server started. Check it with **Settings → AI model → Test the AI**.

**Android:** download **Nourish.apk** on your phone and open it. If it won't install over the old one, uninstall the old app first (your data is safe on the PC).

**iPhone (sideloading):** install `Nourish-unsigned.ipa` with **[AltStore](https://altstore.io)** or **[Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io)**, the same way as before. With a free Apple ID, refresh it every 7 days.

## 🧪 Known limitations
- Your PC must be on, with Nourish running, for the phone apps to work. A plan you already have still shows while it's off.
- Small local AI models sometimes don't follow the "swap a meal" format. Nourish then says so in the chat and changes nothing. Try again, or use a larger model.
- If two devices change the same thing at the same moment, the last change wins.
- **Export meal plan** doesn't work inside the phone apps yet.
