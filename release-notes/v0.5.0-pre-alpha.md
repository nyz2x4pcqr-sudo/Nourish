## 🍳 Complete, checked recipes
Recipes used to come out thin or broken: 2 steps for a 55-minute dish, a step cut off mid-word, "1 cup roasted vegetables", no salt or spices, and calories that didn't match the macros. Now:

- **One meal at a time.** The AI writes each recipe in its own request, so it has room for a whole recipe. This applies to every AI: on the phone, on your PC, Claude and OpenAI.
  - Up to 14 specific ingredients, each with an amount.
  - Up to 8 steps of up to 200 characters, each ending in a full stop. Steps go from prep to plating.
- **Every recipe is checked before you see it.** It is made again (up to 2 more times) if:
  - an ingredient has no amount, or is vague ("vegetables", "meat", "spices");
  - a lunch or dinner has nothing to season it (no salt, spices, herbs or sauce);
  - there are too few steps for the cooking time (at least 3; 4 from 20 minutes; 5 from 40 minutes);
  - a step is cut off, or describes the dish instead of saying what to do;
  - no step says how to serve it;
  - an ingredient is never used in the steps;
  - calories don't roughly match protein×4 + carbs×4 + fat×9 (within 15%);
  - it repeats a dish from earlier in the week.
  - Each remake is told exactly what was wrong.
- **If it still isn't right after 3 tries,** the best try is kept with a **"May be incomplete"** note listing what's wrong, and a **Try again** button.
  - When only the calories are off, they are recalculated from the macros instead.
- **Servings.** Each recipe says **"Serves N"**, using **Settings → Your profile → Servings**. Ingredient amounts are for all servings; nutrition is **per serving**.
- **Progress shows the meal being made:** "Day 3 of 7 · Lunch…", and "remaking (try 2 of 3)" when a recipe is made again.
- **Saved after every meal.** If Nourish is closed mid-plan, it continues from the meal it was on. This now works with every AI, not only on the phone.
- **Swap** and changes asked for in the chat get the same checks.

⏱️ **Plans on the phone take longer:** each recipe is longer, and some are made again. That's the trade-off for complete recipes.

### Fixes in recipes
- **"Preheat oven to 400°F (400°F)" is gone.** When a recipe gives a temperature in both units, it is shown once, in your units. Oven temperatures in °C are rounded to 10, like oven dials: 400°F → 200°C, 350°F → 180°C.
- **One Units setting.** The profile had a second "Units" setting that could disagree with **Settings → Appearance → Units**. It's removed; if you had picked Metric there, Nourish keeps using Metric.
- **A weak AI never stops a plan.** If a recipe still has problems after 3 tries, the best readable try is kept and marked "May be incomplete"; the plan carries on.

## 🔗 Add a recipe from almost anywhere
Tap the **link button** on the Plan tab. There are three ways in: **Link**, **Paste text** and **Screenshot**.

- **Recipe websites.** Nourish reads the recipe data most sites provide, including older sites' "microdata". If a page has none, the AI reads the page's text and pulls the recipe out.
- **YouTube:** the video's description and, when there is one, its transcript.
- **TikTok:** the video's caption.
- **Reddit:** the post and the poster's own comments. If it links to a recipe site, that site is read instead.
- **Pinterest:** the pin's recipe page, or its description.
- **Instagram and Facebook:** the caption, when the post is public without logging in. Most aren't. Nourish then says so clearly and offers:
  - **Paste the text:** copy the caption or recipe and paste it in.
  - **Use a screenshot.** On iPhone, the text is read on the phone itself, so nothing is uploaded. With a Claude or OpenAI key, they can read the screenshot instead.
- **You check it before it's saved.** An editable preview shows the name, meal, servings, time, ingredients, steps and nutrition, plus where the recipe came from.
- **Missing nutrition is estimated** by the AI and marked **Estimated**.
- **The link and site name are kept.** "Original recipe on …" takes you back to the source.
- **Save to Cookbook**, or **Add to plan** on the day and meal you pick. Adding to the plan can save to the Cookbook too.
- **Progress and clear errors**, e.g. "Reading the YouTube post…", "Asking the AI to find the recipe…", "Estimating the nutrition…".

ℹ️ The AI only *copies* a recipe out of the text; it doesn't make one up. If the recipe is only spoken in a video with no transcript, or only shown in the video, there's nothing to copy: use a screenshot or paste it instead.

## ❤️ Cookbook
- **A heart on every recipe,** generated or imported. Tap it to keep the recipe.
- **The book button on the Plan tab** opens your Cookbook.
- **Search** by name or ingredient. **Filter** by meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner) and source (made by Nourish, or imported).
- **Open a saved recipe** to:
  - **add it to any day and meal** (it tells you which meal it would replace);
  - **edit** it;
  - **remove** it.
- **Saved recipes are separate from your plan.** Making a new plan or swapping meals never removes them.
- **Synced like the rest:** with Nourish on your PC, the Cookbook is kept there and shared with every device. Phone-only, it's kept on the phone.
  - ⚠️ Update Nourish on the PC too. An older PC version can't store the Cookbook, so it then stays on each device (the Activity log says so).
