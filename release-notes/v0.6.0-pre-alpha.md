## 🎨 A whole new look
Nourish has been redesigned from the ground up. Everything works the same way; it just looks and feels much better.

- **Dark by default, with a light mode that's just as good.** Warm near-black and cream, with a saffron accent. Change it in **Settings → Appearance**.
- **New type:** an editorial serif for headings (Fraunces) and a clean, friendly font for everything else (Figtree). Both are built into the app, so it works offline.
- **Accent colours have names:** Saffron, Sage, Lagoon, Plum, Rose and Mint.
- **Gentle taps (haptics)** on the phone when you switch tabs, tick things off or flip a switch. They're off when **Reduce motion** is on.
- **New app icon:** a saffron bowl with a sprout, made to stand out on a dark home screen. The launch screen matches.

## 🍳 Recipes are easier to cook from
- A clear **nutrition panel**: calories per serving, with a bar showing how they split between protein, carbs and fat.
- **Tap an ingredient** to tick it off as you go. Amounts are in bold.
- **Tap a step** to mark where you are; tap it again when it's done and the next one lights up.
- **Times and temperatures** in the steps ("25 minutes", "400°F") are highlighted so you can find them mid-cook.

## ⏳ Watch your week being made
While a plan is made, **Today** and **Plan** show a live panel:
- which meal it's working on ("Day 2 of 7 · Lunch");
- a 7-day grid that fills in meal by meal;
- the dishes it has just finished;
- a **Stop** button.

## ✨ Everywhere else
- **First launch:** a proper welcome, with what Nourish does and how to start.
- **Plan:** today's day is highlighted; on small phones and with Large text, time and calories sit under the dish name.
- **Cookbook:** recipes as tiles, showing meal, time, calories and where each one came from.
- **Grocery:** every aisle has its own colour.
- **Chat:** the Nourish mark as your chef.
- **Connect to PC** page restyled to match.

ℹ️ Nothing about how plans, recipes, imports, the Cookbook, the grocery list or syncing work has changed. Your plan, Cookbook and settings are kept when you update.
