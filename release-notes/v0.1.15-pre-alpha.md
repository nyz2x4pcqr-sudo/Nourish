> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

## 🍽️ Eating out
- **In My schedule, any meal can be "I'm eating out":** every day, weekends only, or one day.
- No recipe is planned for that meal and nothing goes on the shopping list for it.
- **Its share of the day's calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber is kept free**, so the meals you cook are sized as usual and the day still adds up.
- Tap an eating-out meal to cook there after all.

## 📥 No more double-saved recipes
- **Importing a recipe that's already in your Cookbook says so before saving.** This works even when the link looks different, for example with tracking tags, `www.` or `m.`, `http`, a trailing slash or `#anchors`.
- Saving it again **updates the copy you have** instead of adding a second one.

## 👩‍🍳 Quick questions for the chef
- Each recipe has one-tap questions: **Lighter**, **More flavour**, **Easier** and **Swap an ingredient**.
- They're asked as questions, so they never change your plan by themselves.

## 🧮 Calorie and allergy fixes
These fixes were rescued from an older copy of Nourish, and each one is tested.
- **Allergies and diets:**
  - Plain protein powder counts as whey: dairy, and not vegan. Pea, soy, rice, hemp or plant-based powders are exceptions.
  - A tree-nut allergy now catches nut or seed butter, mixed nuts, nut milk, nut flour, pesto, praline, marzipan and Nutella. Peanut butter, nutmeg and butternut are still fine.
  - If you avoid olives, you can still have olive oil. Butter lettuce isn't butter.
- **Honest weights:**
  - Prawns and shrimp by the piece are 15 g each. Before, 6 prawns counted as 600 g.
  - A weight in brackets is used: "2 cups (200 g) cheese" was counted as 1,968 kcal.
  - A cup of chicken, mince, fish or grated cheese weighs what it really does.
  - Canned lentils count as cooked lentils.
  - "High protein vanilla yogurt" is a yogurt: 154 kcal, not 599.
- **Breading:** the flour, egg and crumbs a food is dipped in count about a third, which is roughly what sticks.
- **Scaling:** weights in brackets and mixed fractions scale correctly, and "1 kg / 2 lb" scales both amounts.
- **PDF books:**
  - A title that wraps onto two lines is kept whole.
  - Brine and soaking lines aren't counted as eaten.

## 📅 A weekly calorie budget that counts what you really ate
- **A "This week" card on every plan** shows your budget for the week and where you're heading. Snacks, extras and skipped meals are all counted.
- Within ±5% it says **On track**.
- **One tap spreads any difference over the days to come** by resizing portions (75–115% of each day), and you can undo it.

## 🔁 Repeating foods
- A logged food, such as your morning coffee, can **log itself every day or on weekdays**.
- It's added once a day, never before you set it up, and not again on a day you took it off.

## 🔪 Cook mode
- Recipes open **one step at a time in big type**, with the ingredients each step uses.
- **Timers come from the step's own words** ("simmer for 20 minutes") and chime when they're done.
- The screen stays awake while you cook.

## 🧂 Pantry
- **Staples you always have are folded away under the shopping list.** Edit them in Settings → Grocery.
- "A pinch of salt" is now just salt, not an item called "pinch salt".

## 🎨 Look options
- New in Settings → Appearance:
  - **Increase contrast**
  - **Coloured amounts**
  - **Spacing:** compact, comfortable or roomy
  - **Corners:** round, soft or square
- Dark stays the default.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **Logged food could disappear after a reload.** The food log and the activity log were saved under the same name, so each overwrote the other. The food log now has its own place, and your entries are moved over and re-pulled from the PC.
- **Updates no longer mix old and new app files.** Files are sent with "don't cache", so a new app can't run with an old cached theme file.
- "1 cucumber", not "1 cucumbers".

## 🖥️ Better local AI with LM Studio
- **Each recipe the AI writes is held to an exact recipe shape** (LM Studio's structured output), so the answer always reads correctly. It has 3–16 ingredients, 3–10 steps, and whole-number nutrition in sensible ranges.
- **"Thinking" models answer straight away.** Models like Qwen3.5 used to think silently first: over 20 seconds and hundreds of hidden words even for a one-line answer. Turn it back on with the `lmstudio_thinking` setting if you want it (slower).
- **Each recipe has room for about 3,500 tokens** (a token is roughly ¾ of a word), so it's never cut off. In testing, a recipe used 210–470. Anything that reaches a length limit is caught and the meal is made again.
- **"Auto" picks the right model.** With LM Studio's "load models on demand", every downloaded model is listed. Nourish now picks the one that's already loaded, then Qwen3.5 9B (the model tested best for Nourish), then the first in the list.
- **The PC version finds LM Studio even if your settings still use the Docker address** (`host.docker.internal`). Outside Docker, Nourish uses `localhost` instead, so it no longer says "LM Studio not reachable".
- **Tested model:** see the model notes below.
