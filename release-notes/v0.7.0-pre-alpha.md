## 🍽️ Plans made from real recipes, in seconds
Tap **Generate** and Nourish now searches **18 trusted recipe sites, TheMealDB and your own recipe files at once**, picks the best recipe for each meal and sizes it to your targets. There is no "source" to choose any more.

- **Much faster:** finding a whole week takes about **2–7 seconds** (it took about **22 minutes** for the phone's AI to write a week before). The AI now only writes a meal when no recipe fits.
- **"Recipe from [site]"** on every recipe, with a tap to open the original.
- Sites that publish their own nutrition come first, and lighter-cooking sites when your goal is **Lose**.
- A site that is down or busy is skipped quietly; the others still make your plan.
- The **likes** and **avoids** boxes are optional. "All food", "anything", "whatever" or nothing at all means no limits.
- **Swap** picks another real recipe that fits the day.
- See [SOURCES.md](https://github.com/nyz2x4pcqr-sudo/Nourish/blob/main/SOURCES.md) for every site, and why some big sites aren't used (their terms forbid it).

## 🎯 Meals that make sense and hit your numbers
- **Breakfast is breakfast.** No lamb chops in the morning, no oatmeal for dinner, no cake or bread as a meal.
- **Every day lands within about 5% of your calories**, with protein and fat close to target. Portions are sized for you; oil and sugar are cut before portions, and **seasoning is never cut**.
- **Calories by meal** (Profile): **Bigger dinner** (about 25/30/45%, the default), **Even** or **Bigger breakfast**; your own percentages under Advanced.
- **Variety:** no main protein or main vegetable twice in a day, no dish twice in a week, and a mix of cuisines.
- **Flavor is required:** a bland savory recipe gets real seasoning (salt with an amount, plus herbs, spices, citrus or garlic) or isn't used.
- AI-written dish names are spell-checked ("Chiken Tikka Masla" → "Chicken Tikka Masala").

## 🥗 Nutrition you can trust
- Calories and macros are **worked out from the ingredients** using **USDA FoodData Central** data built into the app (works offline). The AI's numbers are never used.
- A site's own numbers are kept only when they agree within 15%.
- Each recipe says where its numbers came from, marks it **Approximate** if an ingredient wasn't found, and lists what wasn't counted.

## 🚫 Avoids and allergies are strict
Everything you avoid, plus your allergies and diet, is checked against every ingredient, including close relatives: avoiding cucumber also rules out pickles and tzatziki (unless you add "pickles are fine"). Typos, plurals, lists and whole sentences are understood.

## 📚 Your own recipe library
Put cookbooks and recipe files (PDF, text, Markdown, saved web pages; photos on iPhone) into the **Recipe Books** or **My Recipes** folder, and Nourish uses those recipes first.
- **iPhone:** Files app → On My iPhone → Nourish.
- **PC:** the Nourish folder next to the app's data (Settings → Recipes → Open my recipe folder).
- **Android:** Settings → Recipes → Add recipe files.

## ⚙️ Settings
- **Recipes:** how plans are made, your recipe library and the nutrition credit.
- **Advanced → Recipe sources & keys:** switch sites on or off, add your own site, choose which sites come first, the optional Spoonacular key, custom calorie split and the AI. Each has a one-line explanation and a **What is this?** note.
- Error messages say what happened and what to do next, in plain words.
