## ✨ What's new in 0.4.0

- **📱 The phone apps work on their own: no PC needed**
  - The iPhone and Android apps now have Nourish built in. Open the app and it works straight away: plans, chat, grocery list, recipe search and links.
  - Your plan, settings and chat stay on the phone.
  - Prefer the old way? **Settings → Server & devices → Connect to my PC instead**. It's still there, just not the default.
- **🧠 AI that runs on your phone**
  - Pick **On this phone** under **Settings → AI model**. It's private, free, and works offline once a model is downloaded.
  - **Checks your phone:** memory (including any extra memory the app is allowed, for example through LiveContainer), free storage and temperature.
  - **Your own top 5, live from Hugging Face.** After checking the phone, the app asks Hugging Face for today's popular and trending chat models, looks up their real file sizes, and picks the best version of each that fits *your* phone. Tags include **Recommended**, **Fastest**, **Best quality**, **Runs cool**, **May get warm** and **Uses most memory**. A different phone gets a different list. Tap **Refresh** to check again; without internet you get a built-in list instead.
  - **One-tap download** with progress. A stopped download continues where it left off.
  - **Search Hugging Face** for any other GGUF model. Every file shows whether it fits your phone.
  - **Sign in to Hugging Face** with a free access token to download gated models (some Llama and Gemma versions). The token stays on the phone.
  - **Plans are made one day at a time**, in a strict format, so small models stay accurate and the phone stays cooler. You see "Cooking day 3 of 7…" as it goes.
  - Chat and "swap Wednesday dinner" work with on-phone models too.
- **☁️ Claude or OpenAI** also work straight from the phone with your own API key.

## 📥 Install

**iPhone** (sideloaded, as before): install `Nourish-unsigned.ipa` with AltStore, Sideloadly or LiveContainer.
- The iPhone app now needs **iOS 16.4 or newer**.
- **LiveContainer:** a raised memory limit is detected automatically, so bigger models appear in your list.

**Android:** install `Nourish.apk`. If it won't install over the old version, uninstall the old app first.
- On Android, models run on the processor only (no graphics chip yet), so they're slower than on an iPhone.

**Windows:** `Nourish.exe` updates itself (**Settings → Updates**). It's only needed if you want to use Nourish from your PC, or share one plan between devices.

## 🧪 Known limitations
- **Keep Nourish open while it downloads a model or makes a plan.** Phones pause apps when you lock them or switch away. A paused download continues when you tap Download again.
- Phone models are much smaller than PC or cloud models. Plans are simpler and may repeat. A 4B model (≈2.5 GB) is a good minimum for good results.
- The speed and time estimates in the model list are rough guesses.
- In phone-only mode nothing syncs with your PC. Each place keeps its own plan.
