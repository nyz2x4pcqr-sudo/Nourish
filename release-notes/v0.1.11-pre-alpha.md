> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

## 📚 Your recipe books are read on iPhone
- **Fixed: books in the Recipe Books folder were never read on iPhone.**
  - An iPhone folder has two spellings, `/var/…` and `/private/var/…`. Nourish cut one from the other, which left "/private" glued to the file's name ("/privateRecipe Books/…"). So every book was "not in the recipe library".
  - Paths are now compared in one spelling, in a normal install and inside LiveContainer.
  - File names with spaces, brackets and accents are tested.
- **Each file shows its status** in Settings → Recipes: waiting, reading (with progress), done with the number of recipes saved, or failed with the reason and a **Try again** button.
- Books can be read again with **Read again**.
- **Add files** shows its progress while big books are copied in. It never blocks the app.

## 🗄️ Book recipes are saved for good
- Every recipe found in a book is saved in Nourish's **offline recipe database**, as a full recipe:
  - ingredients with amounts, steps, servings and times;
  - meal type, cuisine and difficulty;
  - nutrition worked out by Nourish;
  - and the book, author and chapter it came from.
- The database is the phone's own database (IndexedDB), built for thousands of recipes. Recipes from 0.1.10 are moved in automatically.
- **Each book is read once.** It's recognised by its contents, so renaming or moving it doesn't read it again. It's read again only if the file changes, or when you tap **Read again**.
- **The same dish twice** (in two books, or already saved from a website) is kept once.
- **Incomplete recipes** are saved but marked **needs review**. They stay out of plans until you tap **Looks right**.
- **If you delete a book's file,** its recipes stay. Nourish asks before removing them.
- Book recipes **sync with Nourish on your PC**, and you can **export a backup** (My books → Backup).

## 📖 Books get used
- **My books** (in Settings, and from the Cookbook): browse each book's recipes, open one, save it to your Cookbook, or add it to a day.
- **Search:** Cookbook search finds book recipes too.
- **Plans:** book recipes are candidates on equal terms with web recipes, labelled **"From your book: …"**. The log says how many were in the pool and how many were chosen.
- **Chat** knows which books are loaded and answers from the matching recipes.
- **Not just meals:** drinks, desserts and sides from books are kept for snacks, browsing and chat.

## ✍️ Descriptions work again
- **Every description failed instantly.** Its format rule held a hidden character that the phone's AI read as "end of text". Fixed and tested with a real model on the iPhone build.
- When there's no AI, or it fails, the description is made from the recipe itself. For example: "Chicken thighs, new potatoes and lemon, roasted. About 45 minutes."

## 🥄 Amounts
- **Wrong caps fixed:** "½ cup almond milk (vanilla)" is no longer cut to 2 tbsp. The limits for spices and extracts now judge the main ingredient, so vanilla yogurt, ginger ale and tomato paste are left alone.

## 🌐 The recipe library grows again
- **Why it was stuck at 84 recipes:** once your library covered every meal, plans stopped searching. The background refresh only ran after half an hour with the app open.
- **Now:**
  - Every plan also tries a few fresh searches, within the same few seconds.
  - The refresh also runs shortly after the app opens and when you come back to it, on Wi-Fi.
  - The log says when it's waiting for Wi-Fi.
- **TheMealDB** answers are kept for 3 days instead of asking the same searches on every plan.
- **AllRecipes, HelloFresh** and most big sites stay off. Their terms of use forbid automated readers (see the 0.1.10 notes for the full list).

## Not yet
- PDFs still can't be read on Android (EPUB and Word can).
- Recipe sites can change or block apps at any time.
