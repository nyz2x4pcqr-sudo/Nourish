## ✨ What's new in 0.2.0

- **📱 Phone apps (new)**
  - **Android:** `Nourish.apk`.
  - **iPhone:** `Nourish-unsigned.ipa`, which needs sideloading (see below).
  - On first launch they can **find your PC on your Wi-Fi automatically**, and they remember it after that.
  - The apps show Nourish from your PC, so they update whenever the PC does.
- **⬆️ Updates itself:** Nourish.exe checks GitHub when it opens. Under **Settings → Updates**, one tap downloads the new version, checks it against GitHub's fingerprint, installs it and restarts. Your plan and settings are kept.
  - Updating from **0.1.0** is a one-time manual step: download the new `Nourish.exe` below and replace your old one. Updates after this one are automatic.
- **🌐 Recipes from the web:** a new **Web search** source finds real recipes on recipe websites and reads them in full: ingredients, steps, time and, when the site lists it, nutrition.
  - It works with no key through DuckDuckGo, or more reliably with a free Brave Search key.
- **🔗 Add any recipe from a link:** on the **Plan** tab, tap **+ From link** and paste a recipe page. It replaces whichever meal you choose.
- **⚙️ Settings, reorganized:** a clean menu with separate pages: **AI model**, **Your profile**, **Recipe sources**, **Server & phone**, **Updates**, and **Data & privacy**. Each row shows a summary of its current setting.
- **App icon** for the Home Screen, Android and iPhone.

## 📥 Install

**Windows (the PC that runs Nourish)**
1. Download **Nourish.exe** and put it in its own folder.
2. Double-click it and keep the black window open. Your browser opens the app.
3. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**. The app isn't code-signed yet.

You also need an AI, for example [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai) with a model loaded and its server started. Then check everything with **Settings → AI model → Test the AI**.

**Android**
1. Download **Nourish.apk** on your phone and open it. Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser when asked.
2. Open Nourish and tap **Find my PC automatically**, or type the address shown in the Nourish window on your PC.

The APK is signed with a test key, so if a future version won't install over this one, uninstall the old app first. This rarely matters, because the app itself almost never needs updating.

**iPhone (sideloading)**

Apple only allows apps from the App Store, or apps you sign yourself. `Nourish-unsigned.ipa` is unsigned, so install it with a free sideloading tool that signs it with your own Apple ID:
1. Install **[AltStore](https://altstore.io)** or **[Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io)** on your PC and follow its setup.
2. Use it to install `Nourish-unsigned.ipa` onto your iPhone.
3. On the iPhone, enable **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode**. Then trust your Apple ID under **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management**.
4. Open Nourish and tap **Find my PC automatically**. Allow **Local Network** access when asked.

With a free Apple ID, sideloaded apps stop opening after **7 days** until you refresh them in AltStore or Sideloadly. A paid Apple Developer account extends that to a year. Prefer no sideloading? In Safari, open the address from the Nourish window, then tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

## 🧪 Known limitations
- Your PC must be on, with Nourish running, for the phone apps to work.
- Web search can be limited by DuckDuckGo from time to time. Add a free Brave Search key in **Settings → Recipe sources** if that happens.
- Small local AI models repeat meals. A 14B+ general chat model gives more variety than a 7B "coder" model.
- **Export meal plan** doesn't work inside the phone apps yet. Use the browser for that.

See [TROUBLESHOOTING.md](https://github.com/nyz2x4pcqr-sudo/Nourish/blob/main/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) for common problems.
