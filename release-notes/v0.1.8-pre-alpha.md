> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

## 📁 Your recipe files (Settings → Recipes → My recipe files)
- **Add files** button on iPhone, Android and the PC: pick cookbooks and recipes straight from the app.
- The screen shows **where your folders really are**, every file you added, and how far reading it has got.
- Each folder now has a short **Read me** explaining what to put there, and the folders are made the first time Nourish starts.
- **iPhone inside LiveContainer:** iOS shows Nourish's files inside LiveContainer's own folder (Files → On My iPhone → LiveContainer → Data → Application → …), never as a "Nourish" folder. Nourish now tells you the exact place, and **Add files** works either way.

## 📚 Your cookbooks inspire, they don't take over
- Recipes from your files now compete **on equal terms** with every other source.
- Your books are used as **cooking knowledge**: for each meal Nourish finds the few most relevant passages (pairings, seasoning, technique) and gives them to the AI as notes. Pairings your books use (like garlic + lemon) give a small boost to recipes from any source.

## 🍳 Breakfast is breakfast
- Every recipe now has a **meal type, total time and difficulty** (Easy, Medium or Involved, shown on the recipe).
- **Checked in code, not just asked of the AI:** breakfast is real breakfast food in about 15 minutes, lunch is quick and light (about 25 minutes, no roasts or baked pasta), dinner can be as involved as you like. Anything that doesn't fit is rejected before it reaches your plan.
- If nothing fits a slot, Nourish uses a simple, reliable meal (a yogurt bowl, scrambled eggs, a tuna wrap…) instead.

## ⏱️ My schedule (Settings → My schedule)
- Choose how long you have to **make and eat** each meal: 5 min up to No rush, or **I don't cook this meal** (grab-and-go only).
- Different times for the **weekend**, and day by day under Advanced.
- Asked as three quick questions the first time you make a plan (with sensible answers picked and a Skip button).

## 🔢 Honest calories
- **Why every day said exactly 1,500 of 1,500:** each meal's portion was multiplied by whatever odd factor hit its share of the target, and its calories were multiplied by the same factor instead of being worked out again. The totals were forced, not counted.
- Now portions are **realistic** (½, ¾, 1, 1¼… servings, whole eggs, 6 oz not 0.37 lb), calories are **worked out from the amounts as written**, and days land within about 5–10% of your target by choosing the right meals.
- Totals say **about**, and you can tap **How today adds up** and **How is this worked out?** to see every meal and every ingredient.
- **Oil, sauces and toppings** with no amount ("olive oil for drizzling", "parmesan to serve") now count, with a typical amount that's clearly marked.

## 💡 Nourish learns what you like (Settings → What Nourish has learned)
- It notices what you **save, eat, skip, swap away, delete, rate and log**, and what you tell the chef ("I love salmon", "no more mushrooms").
- After a meal: an optional, one-tap **How was it?** 👍 👎, plus *Loved it*, *Too bland*, *Too much work*, *Too small*.
- It learns liked and disliked foods, cuisines, spice level, how much cooking you like, portion size, favourite proteins and how often you want favourites back, and uses them to choose and write meals, still with something new now and then.
- See it all in plain words, **correct or delete** anything, switch learning off, or **reset**. It stays on your phone and your PC; cloud AIs are never told.

## 🐞 Fixed
- On a slow phone, the app could decide the PC wasn't there if the PC took more than 5 seconds to answer at start, and never caught up. It now waits longer, tries twice and catches up later.

## Not yet
- An iPhone app can't make a "Nourish" folder appear in Files while it runs inside LiveContainer; use **Add files** or the location shown in the app.
- PDFs still can't be read on Android.
