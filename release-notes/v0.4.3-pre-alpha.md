> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

## 🐞 Fixed in 0.4.3
- **iPhone: downloads stuck at 0% with no error.** In 0.4.2 the download was handed to iOS's background downloader. On a real iPhone, iOS accepted it and then never started it.
  - Downloads now run as normal downloads, the kind the automated tests prove work.
  - Any stuck download left over from 0.4.2 is cancelled when the app starts.
  - **Keep Nourish open while a model downloads.** The screen stays on by itself during a download.
- **Never stuck without feedback.** While nothing has arrived, the bar says **"Starting…"**. If no data arrives within **30 seconds**, the download stops and tells you to tap **Download** to try again.

## 🔍 Even more detail in the Activity log
- The download's state at 2, 10 and 30 seconds, every 5% of progress, and when iOS finishes or ends the transfer.
- "Waiting for an internet connection" if the phone has no connection.

## ✅ Tested
The automatic tests run on a simulated iPhone. They download **LiquidAI LFM2.5-230M** and a second small model through the app. Each one is checked to the exact byte against the size Hugging Face lists. The test also stops a download partway, continues it, and then makes a full 7-day meal plan on the phone.

## 📥 Install
Same as before: install `Nourish-unsigned.ipa` (LiveContainer, AltStore or Sideloadly) or `Nourish.apk`. Your plan and settings are kept.
