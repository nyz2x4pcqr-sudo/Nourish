> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

## 🔎 Every plan is checked before a release
- A new **plan audit** makes 24 full 7-day plans with the real app, and checks every meal the way a careful person would. The plans use different goals, calorie targets, diets, allergies, budgets and schedules, with and without recipe books.
- **It checks for:**
  - dishes that aren't really meals;
  - meals in the wrong slot, too slow or too much work for it;
  - nutrition that doesn't fit the food;
  - ingredients that are thrown away or bones counted as food;
  - repeats, too many from one site, and expensive food outside dinner;
  - broken descriptions and cut-off titles;
  - odd portions, days off their targets, and avoided foods;
  - too many of Nourish's own recipes.
- **It runs before every release, and a problem stops the release.** Its result is at the end of these notes.

## 🦴 The bone marrow lunch, and why it happened
- **What went wrong:** "8 beef marrow bones" was matched to **lean beef steak**, at 100 g a bone. So 3 lb of bones counted as 800 g of steak: 46 g protein and a "lunch". Bone marrow is almost all fat (85%).
- **Now:**
  - It's matched to bone marrow, and only the marrow inside the bones counts (about a fifth of their weight).
  - It's a **starter**, never a meal. A dish that's mostly fat with little protein is never planned as a meal, whatever it's called.
  - Nourish no longer adds chicken to a starter to make it pass.

## 🥩 Honest numbers
- **Bones and shells aren't food.** Bone-in chicken, ribs, oxtail, pork shoulder, chops, shell-on shrimp, mussels and clams bought by weight count only the part you eat.
- **What's thrown away isn't eaten:**
  - a brine;
  - soaking water;
  - the buttermilk chicken is lifted out of;
  - the frying oil left in the pan (only about a tablespoon a serving soaks in).

  Before, "6 cups oil for deep frying" counted all 6 cups.
- **Cooked rice, pasta and grains count as cooked.** A cup of cooked rice is about 205 kcal, not the 675 of dry rice.
- **New in the food table:** ribs, short ribs, oxtail, bone marrow, plantains, sweet chili sauce, chili crisp, dumplings, salad leaves.
- **Amounts read better:**
  - "½ (15 oz) can", "½ pinch", "2 heads garlic" and "1 gallon" are read correctly.
  - A fish fillet is a portion (about 170 g), not a whole side of salmon.
  - "About 260 g" after a comma is used.
  - Ranges like "¼–1/2 tsp chili flakes" are read. Before, this one counted as 25 g of chili powder.
  - "1-1/2 cups" is one and a half cups.
  - Frozen potstickers, dumplings, gyoza and wontons count by the piece. "24 chicken potstickers" used to count as 24 portions of chicken: a 2,200 kcal soup.
- **Scaled-down recipes stay right:**
  - One portion of a 4-serving recipe no longer rounds a can up to half a can. A small share is given by weight ("3½ oz coconut milk").
  - The weight written in brackets is scaled too.

## 🍽️ Meals that make sense
- **Never planned as meals:** starters, dips, spreads (hummus), coleslaw and sides too light to be a meal.
- **Lunch and breakfast:**
  - A dish that's "Involved" (over 6 of 10) is never at breakfast or lunch, unless your schedule gives that meal plenty of time.
  - Neither is one that must brine, marinate or soak for hours. Overnight oats and other make-ahead breakfasts are still fine.
- **Dinner only:** fried chicken, oxtail, pernil, and fish and chips.
- **Portions are always whole, half or quarter servings.** Resizing twice used to make 0.56 or 1.31 servings.

## 💪 Protein without patching
- **One added food a meal, at most a sensible amount:** 4 eggs, 6 oz chicken or tofu, 2 scoops of protein powder. Never chicken and tofu together. One breakfast used to get 18 eggs.
- **Meals with real protein of their own are chosen first.**
- **When losing weight, the leanest add-on comes first:** egg whites before whole eggs, berries before chia seeds.
- **The added food is a side of its own.** Resizing the dish doesn't change it, so protein and calories don't undo each other.
- **A day still short of protein** with every meal at its most gets a **protein shake** as a snack, said plainly.
- **Vegan and vegetarian:**
  - Vegan breakfasts can get tofu, edamame or soy yogurt.
  - A vegan dish never gets chicken.
- **A day pushed off its calories by the additions** has a meal swapped and is sized again.

## 🌐 Nourish's own recipes are really a backup
- They're only used for a meal when **no** web or book recipe fits it.
- The vitamin and mineral swap and the calorie check use web and book recipes first.
- **No site gets more than 3 meals a week**, including through a swap. Before, sites were named two different ways, so the limit was missed.

## ✍️ Descriptions that describe the dish
- When there's no AI description, the one made from the recipe now leads with the dish and its main food, for example "Pancakes made with cottage cheese, rolled oats, bananas and eggs." It never strings ingredient lines together. The bone marrow used to read "Gallon water, kosher salt and beef marrow bones".
- Descriptions taken from websites drop scraps like "17g salt." and "Recipe video above."

## 📕 Recipe books
- **PDF cookbooks** whose pages all say "Ingredients", "Method" and "Serves 4" lost those lines: they were mistaken for the book's name repeated at the top of each page. So titles and servings were missing, and a 2-serving recipe was counted as 4 (half its calories). Fixed. "Ready in 30 minutes" is read as the time.
- **A scanned PDF on the PC** now says its pages are pictures, and that the iPhone app reads them.
- A dessert in a "Drinks and Desserts" chapter is a dessert, and a drink stays a drink.

## 🚫 Avoided foods
- **Gluten allergy:** buns, brioche, baguette, sourdough, pastry, gnocchi, lasagna and similar are left out. "Gluten-free pasta" and "gluten-free buns" are fine.
- **Vegetarian and pescatarian:** lox and gravlax are fish.
- Plurals ending in "-ies" are caught: avoiding fish also avoids anchovies.
- **Vegan:** soy, coconut and oat yogurts, dairy-free milk, vegan butter and vegan cheese are fine. Milk chocolate isn't.

## Not yet

- Scanned books are only read on iPhone.
