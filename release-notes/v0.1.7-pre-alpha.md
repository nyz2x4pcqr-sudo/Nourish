> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

> **About the version number:** Nourish now uses smaller version numbers. This release follows 0.7.0, and the app knows 0.1.7 is the newer one.

## 🔢 Clean, easy-to-read numbers
Every number (the calorie ring, the dates, the macro amounts, numbers inside headings) now uses even, same-width digits that line up, in every font.

## 🍕 Log anything you eat or drink
- **Add food** sits right under the calorie ring on Today.
- **Type it in plain words:** "2 slices of pizza and a coffee with cream", "a banana", "a can of coke". Nourish works out each item.
- **Search foods**, tap a **recent** or **favorite**, or use a **barcode** (take a photo of it, or type the number; looked up in Open Food Facts).
- **Calories are worked out from USDA food data and typical serving sizes,** never guessed by the AI, and marked as estimates. Change the amount before saving.
- Everything shows under **Snacks and extras**, can be edited or deleted, and counts toward the ring and the macro bars.
- **Mark planned meals as eaten or skipped:** tap the circle on a meal, or choose in the recipe.
- **What's left today, in plain words:** eaten so far, still planned, and free for extras.
- **Over your target?** A calm note, no guilt. It offers to make the rest of today or tomorrow a little lighter, and always asks first.
- **Optional snacks in your plan:** Profile → Meals each day. Choose 0–3 snacks a day (fitted inside your calories), and switch breakfast, lunch or dinner off if you don't eat them.
- The log syncs with your PC and is kept day by day.

## 🎨 Make it yours (Settings → Appearance)
- **Live preview** at the top; changes show everywhere at once, with **Undo** and **Reset to default**.
- **Themes:** Ember (the original), Midnight, Forest, Plum, True black (for OLED screens), Paper, Cloud, or Auto (follows your phone). Each has a few curated backgrounds.
- **Accent colours,** and under **Advanced** any background or accent colour. Text stays readable: if a colour would be hard to read on, Nourish adjusts it a little and tells you.
- **Fonts:** Editorial, Modern, Classic, Rounded and Minimal, all built in, so they work offline.
- **Text size** (Small to Extra large) and **Bold text**.
- **Icon styles:** Outline, Bold, Light, Filled or Sharp.
- **App icon:** six home screen icons to match the themes (iPhone and Android apps).
- **Today screen:** choose which macro bars show. Week start day and units are here too.

## 🐞 Fixed
- An AI-written meal whose calories can't be worked out shows "—" again (since 0.7.0 it could lose its nutrition record).
- Android: choosing a photo in the app (recipe screenshots, barcode photos) now works.

## Not yet
- Barcodes are read from a photo, not from a live camera view, and on Android only where the system can read barcodes. Typing the number always works.
- The PC app and browsers can't change their own icon.
