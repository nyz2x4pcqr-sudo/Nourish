## 🍳 Complete, checked recipes
Recipes used to come out thin or broken: 2 steps for a 55-minute dish, a step cut off mid-word, "1 cup roasted vegetables", no salt or spices, and calories that didn't match the macros. Now:

- **One meal at a time.** The AI writes each recipe in its own request, so it has room for a whole recipe. This applies to every AI: on the phone, on your PC, Claude and OpenAI.
  - Up to 14 specific ingredients, each with an amount.
  - Up to 8 steps of up to 200 characters, each ending in a full stop. Steps go from prep to plating.
- **Every recipe is checked before you see it.** It is made again (up to 2 more times) if:
  - an ingredient has no amount, or is vague ("vegetables", "meat", "spices");
  - a lunch or dinner has nothing to season it (no salt, spices, herbs or sauce);
  - there are too few steps for the cooking time (at least 3; 4 from 20 minutes; 5 from 40 minutes);
  - a step is cut off, or describes the dish instead of saying what to do;
  - no step says how to serve it;
  - an ingredient is never used in the steps;
  - calories don't roughly match protein×4 + carbs×4 + fat×9 (within 15%);
  - it repeats a dish from earlier in the week.
  - Each remake is told exactly what was wrong.
- **If it still isn't right after 3 tries,** the best try is kept with a **"May be incomplete"** note listing what's wrong, and a **Try again** button.
  - When only the calories are off, they are recalculated from the macros instead.
- **Servings.** Each recipe says **"Serves N"**, using **Settings → Your profile → Servings**. Ingredient amounts are for all servings; nutrition is **per serving**.
- **Progress shows the meal being made:** "Day 3 of 7 · Lunch…", and "remaking (try 2 of 3)" when a recipe is made again.
- **Saved after every meal.** If Nourish is closed mid-plan, it continues from the meal it was on. This now works with every AI, not only on the phone.
- **Swap** and changes asked for in the chat get the same checks.

⏱️ **Plans on the phone take longer:** each recipe is longer, and some are made again. That's the trade-off for complete recipes.

## 🐞 Fixes
- **"Preheat oven to 400°F (400°F)" is gone.** When a recipe gives a temperature in both units, it is shown once, in your units. Oven temperatures in °C are rounded to 10, like oven dials: 400°F → 200°C, 350°F → 180°C.
- **One Units setting.** The profile had a second "Units" setting that could disagree with **Settings → Appearance → Units**. It's removed; if you had picked Metric there, Nourish keeps using Metric.
