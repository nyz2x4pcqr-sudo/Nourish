> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

## 🍳 Recipes are cooked as written
- **Nourish never changes a recipe any more.** No added chicken, eggs, egg whites, protein powder, tofu, broccoli, chickpeas or chia, no oil swaps, no trimmed oil or sugar, no added seasoning. 0.1.13 made about 70 such changes in one week.
- **Only the portion changes:** from ¾ to 1½ servings, in quarter steps. A very big day gets a snack or two of its own instead of oversized portions, and if real recipes still can't reach its calories, the day says so plainly.
- **Oil instead of butter** is now a tip on the recipe, not a change.
- A recipe with something you avoid is simply left out instead of being changed.

## 💪 Protein by choosing the right recipes
- Every recipe is scored by **protein per calorie**. With a high target, protein-rich recipes come first for every meal.
- **Searches look for them on purpose:** "high protein" searches, egg whites, cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, lean meats and fish, and the recipe services' protein filters.
- **A day still short gets at most two protein extras.** These are simple items of their own (Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, a protein shake, tuna, hard-boiled eggs, jerky, edamame), shown on the day and on the grocery list.
- **When a day still can't reach the target, it says so honestly**, with the real number. Nothing is faked.
- **A very high protein target for your calories gets a plain note**, with a range that's easier to reach. For example, 150 g at 1,500 kcal is 40% of calories from protein.

## 📊 Fiber, vitamins and minerals are information
- Fiber, vitamin D, calcium, potassium and magnesium appear in a **weekly summary**, with foods that help.
- They never add an ingredient or swap a meal. The optional **Settings → Recipe sources & keys → "Also balance vitamins and minerals"** (off by default) can choose a different recipe for a meal.
- **Tofu was counted wrong:** USDA's entry is a calcium-set tofu with 683 mg of calcium per 100 g. Supermarket tofu has about a third of that, and "1 block" is now 14 oz, not 100 g.

## 🍽️ Meals are meals
- **Drinks are never meals:** Chocomil (Mexican chocolate milk), horchata, lattes, anything mostly milk or juice.
- **Sides are never meals:** a baked sweet potato, rice, roasted vegetables, green beans.
- **Egg dishes go where people eat them:**
  - scrambles, omelettes and huevos rancheros: breakfast only;
  - shakshuka and frittatas: breakfast or lunch.
- **A meal has to stand on its own**, with enough calories and protein of its own.

## 🔎 Every recipe's nutrition is cross-checked
- **Up to three independent figures per recipe:**
  - Nourish's own calculation;
  - the recipe's published numbers;
  - a free service: Edamam, FatSecret or USDA live.
- **They're compared ingredient by ingredient**, so the line that's off shows up: a wrong food match, a bone-in weight, a discarded brine.
- **High** when they agree within about 10%, **Medium** within about 20% (or when two of three agree), **Low** when they disagree, **Estimate** with one source only.
- **Low recipes stay out of plans** until a check settles them.
- **Each recipe says it in plain words:** "Calories checked against 3 sources: they agree", or "Estimate: only one source". Tap to see each source's number and the ingredients that differ.
- **With no keys at all, everything still works.** Each recipe is checked against its own site's numbers, and the app says that's a lighter check.

## 🔑 Recipe and nutrition services (free, optional)
- **A new Settings page** lists, for each service:
  - what it does and a "Free" label;
  - a link to the free key, with step-by-step instructions;
  - a Test button;
  - today's use against its free limit.
- **The services:**
  - **Edamam Nutrition Analysis** (about 400 recipes a month);
  - **FatSecret** (food checks and its own recipes, about 5,000 a day);
  - **USDA FoodData Central** (works without a key);
  - **Spoonacular** (recipes by protein and calories, 50 points a day).
- **Each service's free limit is counted**, and Nourish stops cleanly before it.
- **Each service's credit is shown** where its data is used.
- **Only recipe and ingredient text is sent.**
- **Keys are stored securely:** the iPhone Keychain, the Android Keystore, or encrypted on your PC (keys saved by older versions are moved there). They're never written to the activity log.

## 🔧 Smaller fixes
- **Budget:** the log said recipes were left out for "Any". That stored value has always meant **Normal: no luxury ingredients like wagyu, caviar or truffle**, and the log now says so.
- **Your books:** the log explains why book recipes weren't chosen. Books also get a small preference when they fit as well, within the 3-a-week limit.
- **Recipe sites in the log:** one per line, so the list isn't cut off before Allrecipes any more.
- **Portions:** 1½ portions of a recipe with a bone-in weight in brackets showed 1.9 times the calories. They now stay in proportion.
- **A Parmesan rind** simmered in a broth counts as thrown away.

## 🧪 The plan audit checks more
- **A release is now stopped by any of these:**
  - a changed recipe;
  - a drink, side, appetizer or spread planned as a meal, or a dish in the wrong slot;
  - a portion outside ¾–1½;
  - more than two protein extras in a day;
  - day totals that don't match what's shown;
  - a Low-confidence meal.
- **It reports** how many meals were checked by two or more sources, and how close days get to their protein.
- **A new case uses 1,500 kcal and 150 g protein.**
