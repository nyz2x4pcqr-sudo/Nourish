> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

**0.1.16 includes everything that was planned for 0.1.15** (0.1.15 was never published), **plus fixes found by Nourish's new automatic phone tests and by the plan audit.**

## 📱 Tested on phones automatically
- **Nourish now has phone tests that use the app the way a person does:** first launch, making a plan, logging a snack and a daily coffee, closing and reopening the app, the weekly budget, importing a recipe twice, cook mode, the pantry and the Appearance options.
- They run on an iPhone 16 Pro simulator on GitHub and on an Android emulator. They take a screenshot of every screen, and they found the bugs below.
- **Recipe page:** the "Cook mode" button ran off the right edge of a phone screen, and its hint was squeezed into a column one word wide.
- **Plan screen on bigger iPhones (iPhone 16 Pro):** a long time and calorie line, like "about 46 min + 1 h waiting · 1,236 kcal · 1.5 servings", squeezed the dish name into a column one letter wide. On phones up to 440 px wide, the time and calories now go under the name.
- **Profile:** the long hints next to "Count calories" and "Budget" pushed their menus off the screen.
- **Android 15:** the app was drawn under the status bar and the gesture bar, so the buttons at the bottom of sheets, like "Save to Cookbook", sat under the gesture bar. Android now tells the app how big those bars are, as the iPhone app already did.
- **Importing a recipe a second time** now opens at the top, so the "You already saved this recipe" note is in view. On Android, the keyboard closing after the search no longer scrolls the recipe down to its Save button.
- **Cook mode** no longer lists the olive oil for a step that says "sesame oil".

## 🗓️ More variety in a week's recipes (plan audit)
- **No site gives more than 3 meals a week, even on big calorie targets.** In a 3,200 kcal week only a few sites had dinners big enough. The planner used one of them for early breakfasts and lunches, then had to take a 4th meal from it for a dinner.
- **The planner now plans ahead:** before each day, it works out which sites the rest of the week will depend on for a slot, and keeps their meals for it. Only if no other recipe fits at all does it use a smaller recipe from another site, with a snack to make up the day.
- **Fresh and ground coriander stay two separate ingredients.** They were merged into "6⅛ tsp ground coriander", so the fresh cilantro disappeared from the recipe.
- **"2 T olive oil" is 2 tablespoons**, not 200 g (1,768 kcal), and "1 t salt" is a teaspoon.

## 🍽️ Eating out
- **In My schedule, any meal can be "I'm eating out":** every day, weekends only, or one day.
- No recipe is planned for that meal and nothing goes on the shopping list for it.
- **Its share of the day's calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber is kept free**, so the meals you cook are sized as usual and the day still adds up.
- Tap an eating-out meal to cook there after all.

## 📥 No more double-saved recipes
- **Importing a recipe that's already in your Cookbook says so before saving.** This works even when the link looks different, for example with tracking tags, `www.` or `m.`, `http`, a trailing slash or `#anchors`.
- Saving it again **updates the copy you have** instead of adding a second one.

## 👩‍🍳 Quick questions for the chef
- Each recipe has one-tap questions: **Lighter**, **More flavour**, **Easier** and **Swap an ingredient**.
- They're asked as questions, so they never change your plan by themselves.

## 🧮 Calorie and allergy fixes
These fixes were rescued from an older copy of Nourish, and each one is tested.
- **Allergies and diets:**
  - Plain protein powder counts as whey: dairy, and not vegan. Pea, soy, rice, hemp or plant-based powders are exceptions.
  - A tree-nut allergy now catches nut or seed butter, mixed nuts, nut milk, nut flour, pesto, praline, marzipan and Nutella. Peanut butter, nutmeg and butternut are still fine.
  - If you avoid olives, you can still have olive oil. Butter lettuce isn't butter.
- **Honest weights:**
  - Prawns and shrimp by the piece are 15 g each. Before, 6 prawns counted as 600 g.
  - A weight in brackets is used: "2 cups (200 g) cheese" was counted as 1,968 kcal.
  - A cup of chicken, mince, fish or grated cheese weighs what it really does.
  - Canned lentils count as cooked lentils.
  - "High protein vanilla yogurt" is a yogurt: 154 kcal, not 599.
- **Breading:** the flour, egg and crumbs a food is dipped in count about a third, which is roughly what sticks.
- **Scaling:** weights in brackets and mixed fractions scale correctly, and "1 kg / 2 lb" scales both amounts.
- **PDF books:**
  - A title that wraps onto two lines is kept whole.
  - Brine and soaking lines aren't counted as eaten.

## 📅 A weekly calorie budget that counts what you really ate
- **A "This week" card on every plan** shows your budget for the week and where you're heading. Snacks, extras and skipped meals are all counted.
- Within ±5% it says **On track**.
- **One tap spreads any difference over the days to come** by resizing portions (75–115% of each day), and you can undo it.

## 🔁 Repeating foods
- A logged food, such as your morning coffee, can **log itself every day or on weekdays**.
- It's added once a day, never before you set it up, and not again on a day you took it off.

## 🔪 Cook mode
- Recipes open **one step at a time in big type**, with the ingredients each step uses.
- **Timers come from the step's own words** ("simmer for 20 minutes") and chime when they're done.
- The screen stays awake while you cook.

## 🧂 Pantry
- **Staples you always have are folded away under the shopping list.** Edit them in Settings → Grocery.
- "A pinch of salt" is now just salt, not an item called "pinch salt".

## 🎨 Look options
- New in Settings → Appearance:
  - **Increase contrast**
  - **Coloured amounts**
  - **Spacing:** compact, comfortable or roomy
  - **Corners:** round, soft or square
- Dark stays the default.

## 🐛 Fixes
- **Logged food could disappear after a reload.** The food log and the activity log were saved under the same name, so each overwrote the other. The food log now has its own place, and your entries are moved over and re-pulled from the PC.
- **Updates no longer mix old and new app files.** Files are sent with "don't cache", so a new app can't run with an old cached theme file.
- "1 cucumber", not "1 cucumbers", but without inventing words or foods. "1 bay leaf" (not "bay leave"), and "red chillies" and "sugar snap peas" stay as written. "½ sugar snap pea" was being read as sugar.
- **A dish that's mostly fat with little protein stays out of plans a bit more firmly.** Vegan coconut waffles, 70% fat and 6% protein, slipped into a breakfast.
- **The week's nutrition card on the Plan screen has proper padding** and is now called "Nutrition this week".

## 🖥️ Better local AI with LM Studio
- **Each recipe the AI writes is held to an exact recipe shape** (LM Studio's structured output), so the answer always reads correctly. It has 3–16 ingredients, 3–10 steps, and whole-number nutrition in sensible ranges.
- **"Thinking" models answer straight away.** Models like Qwen3.5 used to think silently first: over 20 seconds and hundreds of hidden words even for a one-line answer. You can turn it back on in **Settings → AI model → Think before answering**, but it's much slower. In tests, a recipe took over 3 minutes and often ran out of room.
- **Each recipe has room for about 3,500 tokens** (a token is roughly ¾ of a word), so it's never cut off. In testing, a recipe used 210–470. Anything that reaches a length limit is caught and the meal is made again.
- **An AI recipe has to be the right size.** A "1 serving" dinner with 800 g of lamb (3,040 kcal for a 900 kcal dinner) used to be kept and shrunk only to ¾, so its day came to 3,013 kcal. Now, if Nourish's own calorie count for a serving is far from what the meal needs (more than a ¾–1½ portion can fix), the AI is told and writes it again. The same check applies when you swap a meal or the AI fills a gap.
- **"Auto" picks the right model.** With LM Studio's "load models on demand", every downloaded model is listed. Nourish now picks the one that's already loaded, then Qwen3.5 9B (the model tested best for Nourish), then the first in the list.
- **The PC version finds LM Studio even if your settings still use the Docker address** (`host.docker.internal`). Outside Docker, Nourish uses `localhost` instead, so it no longer says "LM Studio not reachable".
- **Recommended model for an 8 GB graphics card: Qwen3.5 9B** (`qwen/qwen3.5-9b`, Q4_K_M). It was tested against Gemma 4 E4B and Qwen3 8B on Nourish's own meal prompts, 9–18 meals each:

  | | Qwen3.5 9B | Gemma 4 E4B | Qwen3 8B |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Recipes that read correctly | 18/18 | 18/18 | 9/9 |
  | Meals that broke a rule (made again) | 5/18 | 8/18 | 1/9 |
  | Calories off the meal's target (average) | ~26% | ~30% | ~49% |
  | Ingredients / steps per recipe | 9.6 / 7.0 | 7.8 / 6.6 | 7.7 / 5.8 |
  | Seconds per recipe | 9–11 | 7 | 6 |
  | Graphics memory at 16k context | 6.9 GB | 4.6 GB | 7.0 GB |

  Gemma is faster but kept putting heavy dinners at lunch and repeating the same dish. Qwen3 8B's portions swung from 281 to 1,073 kcal. Settings that fit 8 GB: full GPU offload and 16,384 context.
