> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

## 📏 New: Imperial or Metric
- **Settings → Appearance → Units: Imperial (cups, °F) or Metric (ml, °C).**
  - It starts from your phone's region: Imperial in the US, Metric everywhere else.
  - You can change it any time.
- **Converted everywhere:** recipe ingredients, the grocery list, and amounts inside the instructions.
  - "Roast at 400°F" becomes "Roast at 205°C", and "2-inch pieces" becomes "5-cm pieces".
- **Kitchen rounding**, not calculator rounding: 1 cup → 240 ml, 1 lb → 450 g, 350°F → 175°C.
  - Teaspoons and tablespoons stay as they are, because metric kitchens use the same 5 ml / 15 ml spoons.
- **Your existing plans switch straight away.** The app keeps each amount and unit as the AI wrote them and converts only when showing them, so nothing needs to be made again.

## 🍳 Cleaner recipes
- **No ingredient listed twice in one recipe.**
  - A meal that repeats an ingredient, or has a step like "add ½ cup curry paste and ½ cup curry paste", is made again once.
  - Any repeat that's left is removed, in new and existing plans alike.
- **Impossible amounts are capped.** For example, "1 cup green curry paste" becomes 2 tbsp. The caps:
  - pastes, extracts and spices: at most 2 tbsp;
  - oils and vinegars: at most ¼ cup;
  - salt and baking soda/powder: at most 1 tbsp;
  - fresh herbs: at most 1 cup.
  - Each cap is written to the Activity log, so you can see how often the AI gets amounts wrong.

## 🐞 Fixes
- **Grocery checkboxes are round again.** They were stretched into ovals by the style for text boxes.
- **Grocery aisles:** nut butters and plant milks are under Pantry, and eggplant and green beans under Produce.

## 📥 Install
