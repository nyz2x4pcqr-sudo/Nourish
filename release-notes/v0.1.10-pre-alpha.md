## 🌐 Recipe websites are now the main source
- **More ways to find recipes on a site.** Nourish no longer relies only on WordPress blog search. For each site it picks the best way:
  - the site's own search page;
  - its breakfast, lunch and dinner list pages;
  - its sitemap (the list of every page), read a small part at a time and saved;
  - its RSS feed.
- **New sources that work:**
  - BBC Good Food, BBC Food, olive magazine, Jamie Oliver and SBS Food;
  - Swasthi's Indian Healthy Recipes (breakfasts);
  - British Heart Foundation, Diabetes UK and Diabetes Food Hub.
- **The big sites you asked for were checked one by one.** Most of them don't allow it, so Nourish leaves them alone:
  - **AllRecipes and HelloFresh** (and EveryPlate, Green Chef, Home Chef, Serious Eats, Simply Recipes, Epicurious, Delish, Taste of Home, The Kitchn, Tasty and EatingWell): their terms of use forbid automated readers.
  - **Refuse automated requests:** Blue Apron, Food52, the American Heart Association, Mayo Clinic, USDA MyPlate and Food Network.
  - **No recipes Nourish can read:** Marley Spoon and Dinnerly (behind a login), Gousto (recipe lists drawn by scripts), NHS and NHLBI (no recipe data), Heart & Stroke and Canada's Food Guide (pages missing).
  - **Spend With Pennies** is now dropped too: its robots.txt asks robots to stay away.
- Every site's **robots.txt is respected** while the app runs. A site that refuses Nourish is left alone for a week, then a month. Blocks are never worked around.
- **Star ratings:** recipes show the site's star rating (★ 4.8), and better-rated recipes are preferred.
- **Source and link:** every web recipe still says where it's from, with a button to open the original.
- **Fixed:**
  - Empty searches. "Breakfast tacos" now falls back to the whole text, then the main word, then the site's breakfast list.
  - A moved page no longer stops a whole site.
  - BBC Food recipes were never opened (their web addresses looked like list pages).

## 📚 Your recipe library actually grows
- **Why it was stuck at 84:** the background refresh asked for the same first page of results the plans had already read, so it found nothing new ("0 pages"). It now continues to the next pages.
- **How it grows:** while the app is open, on Wi-Fi, every 3 hours, in small batches. The aim is several hundred web recipes per meal from many sites.
- **New: Settings → Advanced → Your recipe library** shows:
  - how many web recipes are saved for breakfast, lunch and dinner;
  - how many from each site;
  - a **Refresh recipes now** button.
- A plan doesn't wait for slow sites when the library already has enough.

## 🍳 Nourish recipes are the backup
- Nourish's own recipes are used **only when no suitable web recipe is found**, or when you're offline. They're labelled **"Nourish recipe"**.
- **Settings → Advanced → Nourish recipes:** Backup only (the default), Mix in, or Off.

## 🧠 Better judgment of what's a meal
- Recipes are judged by what the dish is, not by single words:
  - "Eggs in Spicy Tomato Sauce" is an egg dish, not a sauce.
  - Chia pudding is a breakfast, not a dessert.
  - Marinated, soy-sauce or boiled eggs are part of a meal, not a meal.
  - A side dish or snack isn't a meal.
  - Chicken porridge is a lunch or dinner, not a breakfast.
  - A roast or pasta bake on a site's "brunch" list is a dinner.
  - A vegetable curry, stew or veggie pie is a dinner, even without meat.

## 📖 EPUB cookbooks (and Word files)
- **EPUB cookbooks can go in the Recipe Books folder, or be added with Add files**, on iPhone, Android and the PC.
- **How books are read:**
  - Chapters are read in order, a little at a time, so big illustrated books don't freeze or heat the phone. Pictures are never loaded.
  - Settings → Recipes shows the progress, with a **Stop reading** button.
  - Recipes are found by their shape: a title, an ingredients list with amounts, and steps. Servings and times are read too.
  - Each recipe keeps its book and chapter ("Easy Mornings · Breakfast") from the table of contents.
  - Introductions, stories, photo pages and indexes are skipped.
  - Book recipes are equal candidates in your plans, with nutrition worked out by Nourish.
- **Word (.docx)** recipe files are read too.
- **Copy-protected (DRM) books** can't be opened. Nourish says so plainly.
- **Kindle books (MOBI, AZW, AZW3)** can't be read. Nourish explains how to convert an unprotected one to EPUB with the free Calibre app.
- Still supported: PDF (iPhone and PC), text, Markdown, saved web pages and photos (iPhone).

## Not yet
- PDFs still can't be read on Android.
- Recipe sites can change or block apps at any time. When they do, Nourish uses your saved library and its own recipes.
