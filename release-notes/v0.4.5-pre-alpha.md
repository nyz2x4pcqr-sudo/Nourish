> ⚠️ **Pre-alpha.** It works, but expect rough edges and changes. Please report problems under **Issues**, ideally with your **Activity log** (Settings → Activity log → Share log).

## 🍳 Better plans from on-phone AI
- **No more junk ingredients.**
  - Each ingredient must now be one short item: up to 48 characters, no commas, at most 10 per meal.
  - Nourish also checks every day it makes. Lines like "ingredients", "use 1 cup" or a whole list squashed into one line make it redo that day once. If junk shows up again, those lines are removed.
- **Fewer repeats.** Each day gets its own cuisine (Mexican, Japanese, Greek…). If a dish is basically one you already have this week, for example "Avocado & Spinach Pancakes" again, Nourish makes that one meal again in another style.
- **Day 7 is no slower than day 1.** The request no longer grows with every dish already planned. It only mentions the last 3 dishes.
- **Hot phone, slower pace.**
  - Nourish checks the phone's temperature before each day.
  - At **serious**, it shows "Cooling down…" and pauses 20 seconds.
  - At **critical**, it stops, keeps the days made so far, and carries on by itself once the phone has cooled down.

## 💾 Plans survive interruptions
- **Every finished day is saved straight away.** If iOS closes Nourish or the phone restarts, the plan continues by itself next time: "Resuming from day 5 of 7".
- **Switching away while it works.** iOS gives apps only a short extra time in the background. Nourish uses that time and writes how much is left to the Activity log. When the time runs out, the current day is paused and continues when you open Nourish again. Nourish sends a notification when that happens, and when the plan is ready.
- The screen stays on for the whole plan. It's switched on once at the start and off at the end, instead of on and off around every day.

## 🛒 Grocery list
- **One row per thing to buy.** For example, "2 large eggs" + "4 eggs" becomes **Eggs ×6**, and "3 oz cooked chicken breast" + "½ cup grilled chicken breast" becomes one **Chicken breast** row.
- Amounts are added up when the units match.
- Junk lines never reach the list.

## 🐞 Fixes
- **Screen titles no longer sit under the clock.** Every tab now keeps its title below the status bar, and a solid strip stays behind the status bar while you scroll.
- **"The model failed while reading the request (code -3)"** came from the phone's graphics chip refusing the work. iOS doesn't allow it while an app is in the background, and it also happens when graphics memory runs out.
  - Now, if this happens in the background, the step waits and continues when you're back.
  - If it happens on screen, Nourish tries once more on the processor.
  - If that also fails, you get a plain explanation instead of a code.

## 📥 Install
Same as before: install `Nourish-unsigned.ipa` (LiveContainer, AltStore or Sideloadly) or `Nourish.apk`. Your plan and settings are kept.
